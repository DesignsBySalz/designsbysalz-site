How to send great photos

Better source photos = better graphics. The quick rules:

Screenshots and re-saved photos lose quality fast. Send the photo straight from the camera roll, or use a Drive/Dropbox link — never text → screenshot → crop.

Aim for photos at least ~1500px on the short side. Zoomed-in crops of far-away action shots usually don't hold up once the background is cut out.

Daylight or bright gym lighting works great. Dark, blurry night shots limit what's possible. If you can, send 3–5 options and I'll pick the strongest.

For cutout-style graphics, the whole athlete (head to cleats) should be in the shot with nothing important cropped off.
